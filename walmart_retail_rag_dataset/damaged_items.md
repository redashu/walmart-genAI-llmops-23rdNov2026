# Damaged, Defective or Missing Items

Document ID: RET-004
Category: Product Issues
Version: 1.0
Status: Synthetic training content

## Item Arrived Damaged

If an item arrives damaged:
1. Ask the customer to stop using the item if it may be unsafe.
2. Ask for a brief description of the damage.
3. Request photos only through an approved support channel, if required.
4. Locate the order and item details.
5. Follow the available return, replacement or support-case workflow.

Do not guarantee a replacement or refund before eligibility is checked.

## Defective Product

For a product that does not work as expected:
- Ask what the product is doing and when the issue started.
- Check whether basic troubleshooting is available in the product documentation.
- Do not recommend steps that could create a safety risk or void a warranty.
- Refer warranty, repair or safety concerns to the appropriate support team.

## Missing Item from an Order

If an order contains multiple items and one is missing:
1. Check whether the order was shipped in multiple packages.
2. Review the tracking information for each package.
3. Confirm which item is missing.
4. Escalate if the item is marked delivered but was not received, or if the shipment is incomplete beyond the displayed estimate.

## Safety Concerns

If a product presents a risk of injury, fire, electric shock or other immediate danger, advise the customer to stop using it and contact the appropriate product-safety or emergency service. Do not attempt to diagnose a dangerous product issue through the chatbot.

## Information Required

- Order number
- Product name or item identifier
- Description of the issue
- Whether the package or product is damaged
- Photos or supporting details only through approved channels
