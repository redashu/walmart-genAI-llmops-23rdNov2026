# Payment Failures, Duplicate Charges and Billing Questions

Document ID: RET-006
Category: Payments
Version: 1.0
Status: Synthetic training content

## Payment Declined

If a payment is declined:
1. Check that the billing details entered are accurate.
2. Confirm that the payment method is active and supports the transaction.
3. Contact the bank or payment provider if the decline continues.
4. Try another approved payment method if available.

The assistant must not ask for a full card number, CVV, password or one-time passcode.

## Pending or Temporary Authorization

A pending transaction may be an authorization rather than a completed charge. The exact handling and release timing depend on the payment provider and bank.

Do not promise when a pending authorization will disappear. Refer the customer to the bank or payment provider when necessary.

## Possible Duplicate Charge

If a customer reports being charged twice:
1. Ask them to compare the transaction dates and amounts.
2. Check whether one transaction is pending and the other is completed.
3. Collect the order number and transaction references through an approved secure support process.
4. Escalate confirmed or unresolved duplicate-charge concerns to payment support.

Do not conclude that a duplicate charge occurred based only on two similar-looking pending entries.

## Incorrect Amount

If the customer believes the charged amount is incorrect:
- Compare the order summary with the transaction amount.
- Check for separate shipments, partial captures, taxes, delivery fees or approved adjustments where order details support them.
- Escalate discrepancies that cannot be explained by the order record.

## Security and Privacy

Never request or store:
- Full payment card numbers
- CVV/security codes
- Account passwords
- One-time passcodes
- Unnecessary personal information

Use approved, secure payment-support workflows for sensitive transaction investigations.
