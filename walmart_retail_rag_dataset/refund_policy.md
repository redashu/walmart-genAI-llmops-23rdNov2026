# Refunds and Refund Status

Document ID: RET-003
Category: Refunds
Version: 1.0
Status: Synthetic training content

## Refund Initiation

A refund is generally initiated after the return, cancellation or other approved refund event has been processed.

The customer should check the order or return status to confirm whether the refund has been issued.

## Refund Destination

When supported by the original transaction and applicable payment rules, refunds are generally sent to the original payment method. The available refund method may depend on the transaction and the payment provider.

Do not promise that every refund will use the same method.

## Refund Processing Time

The time for a refund to appear can vary based on:
- The refund processing status.
- The original payment method.
- The bank or payment provider.
- Weekends, holidays or provider processing schedules.

This training document does not specify a guaranteed number of days. Do not invent a refund timeline. Refer to the order's displayed estimate or the relevant payment provider.

## Refund Not Received

If the customer says a refund has not arrived:
1. Confirm whether the refund is marked as issued or completed.
2. Check the refund reference or transaction status, if available.
3. Confirm the original payment method.
4. If the displayed processing estimate has passed, refer the case to payment support.

## Partial Refunds

A refund may be less than the original order total when only part of an order is returned or when an adjustment applies. Explain only what is supported by the order's itemized refund details.

## Information Required

- Order number
- Return or cancellation reference, if applicable
- Refund status shown to the customer
- Original payment method (never request full card details)
