# Returns and Return Eligibility

Document ID: RET-002
Category: Returns
Version: 1.0
Status: Synthetic training content

## General Return Eligibility

A product may be eligible for return when:
- It is within the return window shown for that product or order.
- The product meets the applicable condition requirements.
- The customer can provide the order details or proof of purchase when requested.

The applicable return window can vary by product category and purchase channel. Do not assume one universal return period.

## Product Condition

Depending on the product and category, the return process may require:
- The product and included accessories.
- Original packaging, where applicable.
- Proof of purchase.
- A description of the reason for return.

Some opened, used, customized, perishable or hygiene-sensitive products may have additional restrictions. Check the product-specific policy before confirming eligibility.

## How to Start a Return

1. Open the order details.
2. Select the item the customer wants to return.
3. Check whether a return option is available.
4. Select the reason for return.
5. Follow the displayed instructions for shipping, pickup or drop-off.

## Return Exceptions

Some products may be non-returnable or subject to special conditions. The knowledge base does not define a complete category-by-category exception list.

If the product category or eligibility rule is not covered, do not guess. Direct the customer to the product-specific policy or a support representative.

## Information Required

To help check a return:
- Order number
- Product name or item identifier
- Purchase date, if available
- Product condition
- Reason for return
