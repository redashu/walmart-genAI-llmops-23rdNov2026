# Delivery, Pickup and Delivery Changes

Document ID: RET-005
Category: Delivery
Version: 1.0
Status: Synthetic training content

## Delivery Estimates

The estimated delivery date and available delivery options are displayed during checkout or in the order details.

Delivery estimates may change due to carrier capacity, weather, address issues, operational delays or other events.

Do not guarantee a delivery time that is not confirmed in the current order information.

## Changing a Delivery Address or Time

Whether an address or delivery time can be changed depends on the order status and delivery provider.

Suggested process:
1. Open the order details.
2. Check whether an edit or reschedule option is available.
3. If no option is available, contact support before promising that a change can be made.

An order that has already shipped may not be eligible for an address change.

## Missed Delivery

If a delivery attempt was missed:
- Review the carrier's latest tracking event.
- Check whether another delivery attempt is scheduled.
- Follow the instructions in the carrier notification.
- Contact support if the tracking information does not explain the next step.

## Pickup Orders

For pickup orders, customers should review the pickup-ready notification and the instructions shown for the selected location.

Do not tell a customer to travel to the pickup location before the order is confirmed as ready.

## Delivery Area and Availability

Delivery availability depends on the item, address and service option. Check the current checkout or order information rather than assuming that every item can be delivered to every address.
